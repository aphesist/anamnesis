Heard the purple finch this morning, for the first time this season. [[Henry David Thoreau|Henry Thoreau]] told me he found the Blue Snowbird (Fringilla biemalis) on Monadnoc, where it breeds. It is never seen here in summer.

Red-winged blackbird sings gurga/ee, and the grackle talks with it hoarsely.
