The invisible connection between heaven and earth, the solitary principle which unites intellectual beings to an account and makes of men moral beings —religion — is distinct and peculjar, alike in its origin and in its end, from all other relations. It is essential to the Universe. You seek in vain to contemplate the order of things apart from its existence. You can no more banish this than you can separate from yourself the notions of Space and Duration.
