To make the omnipresence of God a fact and not a name to the mind, we must look at spiritual laws. The history of the mind is a constant creation. It sleeps on a past law no moment. Let me make a few notes towards a Report of the decisions of this Supreme Court. It is strange that I find no such attempt in all the ages at a digest or even a catalogue of them.
