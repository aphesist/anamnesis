The “Sakoontala” ends with a prayer of the King, —

« And may the purple, self-existent God,

Whose vital energy pervades all space, From future transmigrations save my Soul !”

He persists in his bad opinion of orchards and farming; declares, that the only success he ever had with the farmer was, that he once paid a cent for a russetin apple; and farming, he thinks, is an attempt to outwit God with a hoe: that they — plant a great many potatoes, with much ado ; but it is doubtful if they ever get the seed again.
