If that worthy ancient king, in the schoolbooks, who offered a reward to the inventor of a new pleasure could make his proclamation anew, I should put in for the first prize. I would tell him to write an oration, and then print it, and, setting himself diligently to the correction, let him strike out a blunder and insert the right word just ere the press falls, and he shall know - a new pleasure.

Hateful is animal life resembling vegetable, as when a pear-worm is mistaken for a twig of the tree, or a snake for a stick.

Limitation. — 1 told Mr. Withington at the Medical Rooms in Hanover that this melancholy show of bones of distortions and diseases was one of the limitations which the man must recognize to draw his plan true.
