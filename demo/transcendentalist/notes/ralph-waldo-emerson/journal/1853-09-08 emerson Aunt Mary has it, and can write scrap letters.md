Aunt Mary has it, and can write scrap letters. Who has it, need never write anything but scraps.

[[1853-09-08 thoreau Roses, apparently R. lucida, abundantly out on a warm bank…|Henry Thoreau]] has it.

In this kingdom of illusion life is a dream, in the language of the ancient, — we change only from bed to bed.*...

Henry Thoreau says he values only the man who goes directly to his needs; who, wanting wood, goes to the woods and brings it home; or to the river, and collects the drift, and brings it in his boat to his door, and burns it: not him who keeps shop, that he may buy wood. One ispleasing to reason and imagination; the other not.
