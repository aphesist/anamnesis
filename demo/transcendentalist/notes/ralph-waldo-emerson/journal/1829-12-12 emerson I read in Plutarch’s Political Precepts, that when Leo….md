I read in Plutarch’s Political Precepts, that when Leo Byzantinus went to Athens to appease the dissensions in that city, when he arose to speak, he perceived that they laughed on account of the littleness of his stature. “ What would you do,”’ he exclaimed, “if you saw my wife who scarce reaches to my knees?” And they laughing the more he said, “Yet as little as we are, when we fall out, the city of Byzantium is not big enough to hold us.”

It is strange that the greatest men of the time only say what is just trembling on the lips of all thinking men.
