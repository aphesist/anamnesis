All business relative to performances being thus finished, Br. Hill 2d was chosen committee of one to wait on Br. Gourdin, and inform him that, unless he, in future, attend the meetings of the society more regularly than in times past, he shall be expelled. Br. Blood was likewise chosen committee of one to wait on Br. Lyon for the same purpose. .. .

And now, as my term of service has expired,

I must leave it with him[ Hill, the new Secretary] to transmit to posterity the very interesting proceedings of this society, while I with true firmness of mind (Oh! the sweets of power!), will descend to a private station. So farewell to all my greatness; Frye’s occupation’s gone! Attest, Enocu Frye, Sec’y.
