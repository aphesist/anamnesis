What makes the majesty of Brougham, and Webster and Mackintosh? No brass resembles gold. The consciousness of an innocent life, and the cumulative glory of so many witnesses behinds) .;.

If you would not be known to do anything,

—never do it.27...

The vanishing, volatile froth of the Present which any shadow will alter, any thought blow away, any event annihilate, is every moment converted into the Adamantine Record of the Past,—the fragility of the man into the Eternity of God. The Present is always becoming the Past. We walk on molten lava on which the claw of a fly or the fall of a hair makes its impression, which being received, the mass hardens to flint and retains every impression forevermore.
