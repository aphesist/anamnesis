We have had two peerless summer days after all our cold winds and rains. I have weeded corn and strawberries, intent on being fat, and have foreborne study. The Maryland yellow-throat pipes to me all day long, seeming to say Extacy ! Extacy! and the Bob-o’-Lincoln flies and sings. I read during the heat of the day Beppo and Manfred. What famine of meaning! Manfred is ridiculous for its purposeless raving, not all the genuine love of nature, nor all the skill of utterance can save it. It is all one circular proposition.
