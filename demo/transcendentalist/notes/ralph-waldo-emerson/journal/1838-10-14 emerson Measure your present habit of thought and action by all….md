Measure your present habit of thought and action by all your external standards, if you will; by the remembrance of your dead ; by the remembrance of the three or four great men who are yet alive; by the image of your distant friends; by the lives and precepts of the heroes and philosophers ; these all are only shadows of the primary sentiment at home in your old soul.

The talent of the poet seems to consist in presence of mind, the ability to seize the fact and image which all others know very well, but cannot collect themselves sufficiently to use in the right time.
