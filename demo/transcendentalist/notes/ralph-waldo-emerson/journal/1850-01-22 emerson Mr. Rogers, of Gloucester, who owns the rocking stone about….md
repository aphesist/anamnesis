Mr. Rogers, of Gloucester, who owns the rocking stone about half a mile from Gloucester village, which stone the tide moves, told mea story of Jarvis, of the Sandwich Glass Company, who came down there with his gun, and bonded all the farms, the farmers thinking him a crazy man with a pocket full of money: so they followed him, and got each man ten dollars from him for a bond, and, last of all, his landlord also got a gold watch and five dollars. Then Jarvis went up to Boston, put himself in funds, came down and demanded a deed of all these lands and houses, to the terror of the owners.
