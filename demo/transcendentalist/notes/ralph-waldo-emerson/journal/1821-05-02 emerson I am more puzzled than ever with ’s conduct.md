I am more puzzled than ever with ’s conduct. He came out to meet me yesterday, and I, observing him, just before we met, turned another corner and most strangely avoided him. This morning I went out to meet him in a different direction, and stopped to speak with a lounger, in order to be directly in "§ Way} but turned into the first gate and went towards Stoughton. All this [took place (?)] without any apparent design and as [soberly (?) ] as if both were intent on some tremendous affair."

May tioth.

Huzzafor my Magician! he engages me finely.’ I am as interested in the tale and as anxious to know the end as any other reader could be. By the by, this tale of mine might be told with powerful effect by a man of good voice and natural eloquence.
