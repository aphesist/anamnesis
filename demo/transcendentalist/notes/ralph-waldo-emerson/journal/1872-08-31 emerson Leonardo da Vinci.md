THE NILE ROME, FLORENCE, PARIS THREE WEEKS OF LONDON OXFORD RETURN HOME

1873 (From Journal ST)

At Cairo, he was pleased to find Mr. Bancroft, and went about the city with him. Mr. Charles G. Leland and Mr. Augustus Julius Hare were also agreeable. General Charles Stone (formerly of the United States Army, then Chief-of-Staff of the Turkish force in Egypt)

and his family showed great courtesy to Mr.

Emerson and his daughter. |

SHEPHEARD’S HorTEL, Cairo, January, 1873.

Nothing has struck me more in the streets ~ here than the erect carriage and walking of the Copts (I suppose them); better and nobler in figure and movement than any passengers in our cities at home.
