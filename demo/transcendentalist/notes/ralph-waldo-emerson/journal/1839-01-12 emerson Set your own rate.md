Set your own rate." ...

Let us call Goose Pond the Drop, or God’s Pond. [[Henry David Thoreau|Henry Thoreau]] says, “ No; that will shock the people; call it Satan’s Pond and they will like it, or still better, Tom Wyman’s Pond.” Alas! say I, for the personality that eats us up.

“Seekest thou great things? Seek them not.” — JEREMIAH, XIV, 6. February 3. Returned last night from Plymouth, where on Thursday evening, 31 January, I read a lecture on Genius; on Friday afternoon, one on Home; and in the evening, one on Being and Seeming.
