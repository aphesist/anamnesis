It is not for nothing, that very few heads are sent into the world busy with abstractions, and very many heads busy with making money. Accept the order of the world, though it make you a shopkeeper.

That is one thing; the second thing which comes often to mind, lately, is, the conviction that our security is in the reactive force which develops all kinds of energy out of the same time.
