1874 (From Journal ST)

The Journal was almost entirely neglected. The following entry is the first, and must have been in latter March, after receiving the letter from Judge Hoar which follows it.]

Charles Sumner. For Sumnet’s merit, go back to the dark times of 1850 and see the position of Boston and its eminent men.
