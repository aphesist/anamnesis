© All which we call invention, discovery, in the higher sense, is the important practice or setting at work of an original perception of truth, which, long formed in silence, unexpectedly, as with lightning speed, leads to a useful cognition. It is an opening that is made from the Inner to the Outward which lets man anticipate his resemblance toGod. Itis asynthesis of the World and the Spirit which gives the most blessed assurance of the eternal harmony of Being.” GoeETHE, vol. xxi, p. 248.

“‘ Literature is the fragment of fragments. The least of what was done or said was written. Of what was written the least part has remained.”

“The question Whence had the poet this? refers to the What not the How.” Ibid.
