In town, a stout soldier, an Irishman, walked before me, large, and with all too much motion. A little boy stopped him, “Please give me a cent.’ Soldier stooped to find out what he said, and then, with unfeigned astonishment, said, “A cent,—Great God! I give youa cent!” and rushed indignantly forward.
