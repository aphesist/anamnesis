But is not every effort that her sons make to advance the intellectual interests of the world, and every new thought which is struck out from the mines of religion and morality, a forward step in the path of her greatness? Peace be with her progressive greatness, —and prosperity crown her giant minds. A victory is achieved today for one,! whose name perchance is written highest in the volume of futurity.

1 Webster was chosen representative to Congress by a majority of 1078 votes this morning. (R. W. E.)
