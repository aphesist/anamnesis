henry david thoreau (1817–1862), surveyor, naturalist and writer of concord, massachusetts. he kept a journal from october 1837 until november 1861.

in this brain: his journal (1906 edition), [[Walden]], [[A Week on the Concord and Merrimack Rivers]], Walking and [[On the Duty of Civil Disobedience]].
