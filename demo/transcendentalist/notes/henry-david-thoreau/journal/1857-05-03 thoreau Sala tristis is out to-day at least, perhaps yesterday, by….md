Sala tristis is out to-day at least, perhaps yesterday, by what I may call S. tristis Path. Viola ovata is pretty common there.

Above the Cliffs, scare up a pair of turtle doves from the stubble, which go off with their shrill rattling whistle. Corydalis glauca is five inches high. The pistillate Equisetum arvense shows itself.

To-day we sit without fire.

[[Ralph Waldo Emerson|Emerson]] says that Brewer tells him my “night warbler” is probably the Nashville warbler.
