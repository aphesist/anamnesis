A catbird’s nest eight feet high on a pitch pine in [[Ralph Waldo Emerson|Emerson]]’s heater piece, partly of paper. A summer yellowbird’s, saddled on an apple, of cotton-wool, lined with hair and feathers, three eggs, white with flesh-colored tinge and purplish-brown and black spots. Two hair-birds’ nests fifteen feet high on apple trees at R. W. E.’s (one with two eggs). A robin’s nest with young, which was lately, in the great wind, blown down and somehow lodged on the lower part of an evergreen by arbor, — without spilling the young!
