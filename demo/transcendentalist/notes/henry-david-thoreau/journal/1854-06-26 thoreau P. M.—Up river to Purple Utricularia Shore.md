P. M.—Up river to Purple Utricularia Shore.

Cornus sericea, yesterday at least. Small front-rank polygonum, a smut-like blast in the flower. Small form of arrowhead in Hubbard’s aster meadow, apparently several days. I am struck, as I look toward the Dennis shore from the bathing-place, with the peculiar agreeable dark shade of June, a clear air, and bluish light on the grass and bright silvery light reflected from fresh green leaves. Sparganium, apparently ramosum, two or three days. The largest apparently the same, but very rarely in blossom ; found one, however, with a branched scape, but not concave leaves except below. Gratiola. Cicuta maculata, apparently to-morrow. )
