Walked to Walden and Fair Haven Hill with Mrs. Wilson and son, of Cincinnati. ‘They tell me that the only men of thought in that part of the world are one young Goddard and Stallo the German. The subjects that engage the mass are theological dogmas and European politics. The man of the West is not yet.

Solidago bicolor, white goldenrod, apparently in good season.

Aug. 138. Mirkania scandens well out; was not out July 18th. How long since, then? Perhaps not far from Ist August. The Lactuca sanguinea (var.) was perhaps as early as the other. Rhexia, very common on those bare places on the river meadows from which the soil has been moved by the ice. Saw the head and neck of a great bittern projecting above the meadowgrass, exactly like the point of a stump, only I knew there could be no stump there. There are green lice now on the birches, but I notice no cotton on them. Pennyroyal abundant in bloom.
