This morning the first frost. Yet the 10th was one of the warmest days in the year. Methinks it is the Amaranthus hypochondriacus, prince’s-feather, with “bright red-purple flowers’ and sanguine stem, on [[Ralph Waldo Emerson|Emerson]]’s muck-heap in the Turnpike, and the Polygonum orientale, prince’s-feather, in E. Hosmer’s grounds. Blue vervain still. The grass is very green after the rains, like a second spring, and, in my ride yesterday, the under sides of the willows, etc., in the wind, the leaves of the fall growth perhaps, reminded me of June. Is not the colder and frosty weather thus introduced by a rain? 2. e. it clears up cold.
