Cerasus pumila in full bloom. How long? Bank swallows — ashy-brown above — have holes at Deep Cut. Have not surely distinguished them before, this season. Sage willow may have begun to leaf a week or ten days ago or more. Cuckoo. Scared up a nighthawk — from the white on wings — amid the dry leaves on the edge of a copse on Fair Haven Hill, where apparently it had been scratching, the leaves looking as if they had been turned up. Linaria Canadensis on Cliffs open. The deciduous trees leafing begin to clothe or imvest the evergreens. The oaks are a little more than in the gray. Huckleberry open, possibly yesterday. Fringed polygala, how long? Herd’s-grass (?) on Channing’s bank, pollen.t Harris tells [[Ralph Waldo Emerson|Emerson]] my cicada is the Noveboracensis (?), known to New-Yorkers. Lupine not open yet for two or three days. Not yet chinquapin oak.
