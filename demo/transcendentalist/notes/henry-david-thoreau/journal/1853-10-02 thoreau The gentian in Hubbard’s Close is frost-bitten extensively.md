The gentian in Hubbard’s Close is frost-bitten extensively. As the [witch-] hazel is raised above frost and can afford to be later, for this reason also I think itis so. ‘The white pines have scarcely begun at all to change here, though a week ago last Wednesday they were fully changed at Bangor. ‘There is fully a fortnight’s difference, and methinks more. The [witch-] hazel, too, was more forward there. There are but few and faint autumnal tints about Walden yet. The smooth sumach is but a dull red.
