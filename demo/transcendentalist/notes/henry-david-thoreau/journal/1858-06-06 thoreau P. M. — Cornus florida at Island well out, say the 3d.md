P. M. — Cornus florida at Island well out, say the 3d. I hear of linnea out in a pitcher and probably (?) in woods.

Go to Painted-Cup Meadow via Assabet Bath.

See three or four Emys insculpta about, making their holes in the gravelly bank south of Assabet Bath, and a few holes which must have been made a day or two, probably by the same. Golden senecio is not uncommon now. Am surprised to find that the buckbean flowers are withered, being killed by the recent frosts. Yellow Bethlehem-star.

Edith [[Ralph Waldo Emerson|Emerson]] has found, in the field (Merriam’s) just south of the Beck Stow pine grove, Lepidiwm campestre, which may have been out ten days.
