Surveying for Stow in Lincoln.

Two-leaved Solomon’s-seal. I hear of young song sparrows and young robins since the 16th. That handsome spawn of Ed. [[Ralph Waldo Emerson|Emerson]]’s aquarium — minute transparent ova in a double row on the glass or the stones — turns out to be snail-spawn, it having just hatched, and there was no salamander-spawn, as I thought on the 18th of April. Not Paludina decisa, but the smaller and simpler one.
