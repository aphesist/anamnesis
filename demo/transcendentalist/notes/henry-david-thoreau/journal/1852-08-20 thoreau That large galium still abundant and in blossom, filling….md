That large galium still abundant and in blossom, filling crevices. The Corallorhiza multiflora, coral-root (not odontorhiza, I think, for it has twentyfour flowers, and its germ is not roundish oval, and its lip is three-lobed), by Brister’s Spring. Found by [[Ralph Waldo Emerson|R. W. E.]], August 12; also Goodyera pubescens found at same date. The purple gerardia is very beautiful now in green grass, and the rhexia also, both difficult to get home. I find raspberries still. An aster with a smocth leaf narrowed below, somewhat like A. ampleaicaulis (or patens (Gray)?). Is it var. phlogifolius?

Is that smooth, handsome-stemmed goldenrod in

Brown’s Sleepy Hollow meadow Solidago serotina ? Bidens, either connata or cernua, by Moore’s potatofield.
