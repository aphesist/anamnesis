A. M. — To Hill.

The hedges on the Hill are all cut off. The journals think they cannot say too much on improvements in husbandry. It is a safe theme, like piety. But for me, as for one of these farms brushed up, — a model farm, — I had as lief see a patent churn and a man turning | it. It is simply a place where somebody is making money.?

I see a snow bunting, though it is pleasant and warm.
