A beautiful and warm day. I go to build an arbor for [[Ralph Waldo Emerson|R. W. E.]] The thrasher has been heard this morning. While at work I hear the bobolink and, methinks, peetweet along the brook

(surely see it on the 9th). Sugar maple by Dr. Barrett’s, possibly to-day.
